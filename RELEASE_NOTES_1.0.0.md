# Typing Tape 1.0.0

Release date: October 21, 2026.

The first public release of Typing Tape, a calm typing-speed trainer for Windows: one endless
tape of text scrolling past a fixed caret. The full list of features is in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Good to know

- **Unsigned.** The installer and the app are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn
  and Smart App Control may block them. See [docs/INSTALLATION.md](docs/INSTALLATION.md).
- **License.** Typing Tape is freeware under [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt): free for personal and
  commercial use, free to share unmodified and free of charge, not for sale, no modified
  versions without permission, source code not included. `LICENSE.txt` and
  `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` are installed next to the app (and removed by the uninstaller) and
  are included in the portable ZIP, in the `Typing Tape` folder.
- **Your data** stays on your PC and is kept when you uninstall, unless you choose to delete it.
  See [docs/PRIVACY.md](docs/PRIVACY.md).
